An internal company podcast gives corporate and teams a more flexible way to share updates, training, leadership messages, onboarding material, and culture stories. Employees can listen while commuting, traveling, walking, or working through routine tasks. For remote teams, this can make company communication feel more personal than yet another all-hands meeting, email or presentation.
