Podcast listeners are, obviously, used to advertising as part of the shows they consume. Edison Research’s Podcast Consumer 2025 found that 88% of US weekly podcast consumers age 13 and older agreed that ads are a fair price for free content. Another 68% said they did not mind podcast ads.
