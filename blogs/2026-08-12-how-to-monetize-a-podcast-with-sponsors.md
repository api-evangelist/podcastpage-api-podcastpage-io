Podcast audio CPM prices (cost per thousand downloads) normally fall within the $10 to $50 range. This depends on your niche, the ad-slot (pre-roll, mid-roll etc.), number of slots per episodes and more. We’ve seen some podcasters who also charge in a much higher range – so it also depends on demand.
